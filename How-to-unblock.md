# How to unblock

### Eyy! I see you visited this to learn how to unblock this website on your school chromebook!
### Cool, anyway, let's get into it.
### If you can learn it by just reading, this is for you. If not, still read this, but at the sametime, see pictures in the folder in the repo titled "How-to-unblock-pictures"
#### Step 1, Go to your Schoology homepage.
#### Step 2, Click the calendar icon. (looks kinda like this 📆)
#### Step 3, Click on any blank square. (its a square with a number in the top left corner)
#### Step 4, A menu pops up, in the title, write something like "Unblocked games" or something.
##### Step 4.A, In the description, write "ABO", short for about blank opener, or really anything, it doesn't really matter.
##### Step 4.B, Under the description box, there are 4 buttons, "File," "Link," "Resource," and "Audio/Video Recording."
##### Step 4.C, Press the "Link" button, represented by a chainlink icon
##### Step 4.D, Another menu pops up, titled, "Attach Link/Embed."
##### Step 4.E, Where it says "Link/Embed:" copy-paste this HTML code: <iframe src="https://haqwhd.github.io/About-blank-opener/" width="500" height="1000" iframe>
##### Step 4.F, In the "Title:" section of the "Attach Link/Embed" menu, write something like "Unblocked Games."
##### Step 4.G, Click the (probably blue) "Attach" button.
##### Step 4.H, Click "Preview" to make sure it works, it'll show a popup that shows the About-blank-opener site. That means it's working. Close the preview after you know it works, or you've gone back to fix why it didnt work.
#### Step 5, Click the (probably blue) "Create" button. This takes you back to the calendar view you were in once you did the second step.
#### Step 6, Click on the new event that you just created under my instruction, and click "View Item."
#### Step 7, This takes you to the full event page that shows all the content, (the title, the description, and the embed you made, the About-blank-opener).
#### Step 8, Bookmark this page by clicking the star on the far right of the URL bar at the top of the browser screen.
### Now, whenever you want to use the site on your school chromebook, click the bookmark, and click on the embed icon. (looks kind of like 2 frames of film with a play button in the bottom-right corner)
